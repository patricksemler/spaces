# Recording the video pitch (about 2:30)

Use the Devpost video outline from SUBMISSION.md. The prototype covers the demo section, roughly 1:30 of the video.

## Setup (2 min)
1. Use Chrome on a laptop, full screen (⌃⌘F), with the window at 16:9 if you can. Open https://spaces.patricksemler.dev
2. Press **H** to hide the step bar and captions. The keyboard shortcuts still work while they're hidden.
3. Press **1** to reset to beat 1 (Today).
4. Start the screen recorder (QuickTime → New Screen Recording, or ⌘⇧5) and record the browser window.
5. Record your voiceover live or add it afterward. Each beat runs its taps on its own, so all you do is press **→**.

> Prefer the captions on screen? Skip step 2. They match the voiceover lines below.
> For hands-free recording, press **Space** (autoplay). It plays each beat, holds about 3 s, and moves on. All 6 beats take about 75 s.

## Tips
- Pressing **→** before a beat finishes jumps straight to the next one, and the previous beat's end state is applied instantly. If you miss a step, press **←** (it shows the previous beat's finished state), then **→** to replay.
- Stills for slides are in `screenshots/` (1920×1080).
- Deep link to any finished state: `?beat=4&static=1&clean=1`
